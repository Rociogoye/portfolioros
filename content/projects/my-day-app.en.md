## The project

**My Day App** is a published web app for organizing the day flexibly, tracking progress, and reviewing how energy is distributed across work, training, study, and personal life.

The product focuses on a calmer experience: users can work through small steps, switch between today, planning, logging, and review views, and keep their data locally in the browser.

## My role

- Defined the product proposal and application architecture.
- Designed the main planning, logging, and review flows.
- Built the responsive interface with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Added states, filters, metrics, dark mode, and local persistence.
- Published the app and maintain the codebase on GitHub.

## Main features

- Daily tasks and priorities.
- Date and time-block planning.
- Habit, energy, and notes logging.
- Visual progress review.
- Labels for different areas of life.
- Light and dark modes.
- Local persistence without a mandatory account.

## UX decisions

- Reduce cognitive load through four clear views: Today, Plan, Log, and Review.
- Turn large goals into concrete, manageable steps.
- Use a calm visual hierarchy so the app does not feel like another source of pressure.
- Keep the experience lightweight and accessible on any device.

## Technologies

Semantic HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Tailwind CSS, and browser local storage.
